# CRYPTOBUDS: THE CANNAVERSE — Full Quest Outline (draft for approval)
### "Where does the weed come from?"

---

## THE THESIS
You are a bud. You smoke weed. Weed is buds. Nobody asks.

One bud asks. That's the game.

You follow the supply chain up — through the corners, through the corporations, into the **CANNABULS** — and you find out the whole economy runs on buds being *grown, sourced, sedated and sold for consumption*. You expose it. You beat every one of them.

**And nothing happens.** No convictions. The files come out, everybody reads them for a week, and the machine re-staffs. That's the ending. That's the point.

> **The feeling we're after:** *"Damn, this hit a real moment"* — and also *"damn, this is fun and this is weed."* Boondocks: you laugh the whole way in, and the laugh dies in your throat at the end.

---

## THE THREE LAYERS OF THE MACHINE
The conspiracy is a pyramid. Each act peels one layer.

**1 · THE CORNERS** *(street level — Acts 1–3)*
**Team Red Haze** vs **Team Blue Dream.** They think they're at war over territory. Neither owns a single block. They're both unknowingly *franchisees* — they move product up, and the beef keeps them too busy to look up. The saddest reveal in the game: the same accountant sends both gangs their rent.

**2 · THE CORPORATIONS** *(the legitimate face — Acts 4–6)*
**Verdant Wellness Group** — the dispensary chain gentrifying every block you've walked through. Clean stores, neon leaf signs, "farm-to-table" marketing. They're the laundering layer: they turn sourced buds into shelf product with a barcode and a wellness slogan. Their sin isn't violence — it's *paperwork*. And FIRE (the feds) checks their permits and raids your block instead.

**3 · THE CANNABULS** *(the top — Acts 7–8)*
The Elite. They don't sell weed. They **source** it. They decide which buds get "harvested." Old money, private islands, a client list. Everyone in the Cannaverse half-knows they exist and nobody says the name out loud. Beating them changes nothing, because they were never the machine — they were just this decade's staff.

*(Etymology in-world: nobody knows if "Cannabuls" is a family name or a description. That ambiguity is the joke.)*

## ACT-BY-ACT

### ACT 1 — "It's Just Weed" *(Dubsack Block → Kush Hollow)*
**Tone:** pure comedy. Nothing is wrong yet. This act is *funny* so the rest can hurt.
- **Q1.1 First Bud** — Old Malawi gives you a starter. He's long-winded, everyone rolls their eyes.
- **Q1.2 Corner Run** — Kush needs a resupply from the Corner Store. Pop's rent went up again. Comedy beat: everything in the store costs more than last week.
- **Q1.3 The Grow Rows** — get to Kush Hollow. Catch your first buds.
- **Q1.4 Smoke-Off: Farmer Haze** → **Soil Props**
- **REVEAL:** Haze mentions offhand that the farm's harvest hasn't gone to local blocks in *years.* "We ship it out. Where? Man, I just grow it." Sour D's line: *"Then who's growing what WE smoke?"*
- **Gate:** need Soil Props to head into the Backstreets.

### ACT 2 — "Ask The Plug" *(Backstreets → Southside)*
**Tone:** street comedy, first shadow.
- **Q2.1 Follow the Product** — trace a bag back to The Plug.
- **Q2.2 The Plug's Errand** — he'll talk if you do him a favor (three Team battles).
- **Q2.3 Smoke-Off: Big Kush** → **Block Props**
- **REVEAL:** The Plug genuinely *doesn't know* his supplier. Product arrives, money leaves. He's never met anyone. First real chill: the guy at the "top" of the block is a middleman too.
- **Comedy:** Big Kush explaining he "owns" a block he rents.

### ACT 3 — "The Beef" *(Blue Block ↔ Red Row)*
**Tone:** the funniest act, and the saddest.
- **Q3.1 / Q3.2** — both gangs recruit you. You run errands for each, hearing identical grievances in different slang.
- **Q3.3 Bookkeeping** — you find a ledger. Both gangs' payments route to the same address.
- **Q3.4 Smoke-Off: Ruby & Cobalt** (a rare double battle — the two lieutenants finally teaming up)
→ **Truce Props**
- **REVEAL:** the beef is *managed.* Somebody keeps it running to keep the corners distracted. The address on the ledger: **Verdant Wellness Group.**
- **Payoff line (Cuz):** *"We been fighting three generations over a corner neither of us own."*

### ACT 4 — "Peer Reviewed" *(Sativa State → Terp Valley)*
**Tone:** academia satire → real horror.
- **Q4.1 Campus Plug** — the student dealer's product tests weird.
- **Q4.2 The Lab** — run a terpene analysis. It comes back with *bud DNA* — not plant. Bud.
- **Q4.3 The Buried Paper** — a grad student published this. The paper was pulled. She's "on leave."
- **Q4.4 Smoke-Off: Professor Terp** → **Scholar Props** (he knew; tenure was the bribe)
- **REVEAL:** the product is *people.* Confirmed, in writing, years ago. Nobody ran it.
- **Gate:** Terp Trail needs 4 Props.

### ACT 5 — "Nights at the Port" *(Acapulco City → Port Reup)*
**Tone:** money and glamour hiding logistics.
- **Q5.1 The Promenade** — Acapulco is beautiful and everything is bought.
- **Q5.2 3AM** — stake out the docks. Containers arrive. No manifest. Dockman: *"I load what they tell me. That's how you keep a job and a pulse."*
- **Q5.3 Manifest** — steal the paperwork. Shipper: a shell. Consignee: Verdant Wellness Group.
- **Q5.4 Smoke-Off: Goldie** → **Gold Props**
- **REVEAL:** volume. This isn't a side trade — it's *the* supply chain.

### ACT 6 — "Wellness" *(Neon Row → Hashbury)*
**Tone:** the corporate act. Cheerful branding over atrocity.
- **Q6.1 The Pitch** — VWG recruits *you*. Brand ambassador. Signing bonus. Genuinely tempting.
- **Q6.2 Back of House** — behind the dispensary: intake, sedation, sorting. Named product. **"Exotics"** are buds who were *someone*.
- **Q6.3 The Whistle** — you get the footage out. It trends for a day. Nothing happens.
- **Q6.4 Smoke-Off: Velvet** (VWG's regional director) → **Neon Props**
- **REVEAL:** exposure doesn't work. Everyone already half-knew. **This is the game's mid-point thesis and the first taste of the ending.**
- **Comedy:** VWG's crisis-PR statement is a masterpiece of saying nothing.

### ACT 7 — "The List" *(Landrace Ridge → Trichome Heights)*
**Tone:** old money, cold, quiet. The comedy gets very dry.
- **Q7.1 The Elders** — Landrace Ridge remembers the world before. They're the last unbought.
- **Q7.2 Invitation** — you're let into the Heights *as entertainment.*
- **Q7.3 The Client List** — it exists. It's mostly people you've already met being polite to you.
- **Q7.4 Smoke-Off: Lady Frost** → **Crystal Props**
- **REVEAL:** Frost is on the list and doesn't deny it. *"Everyone here is on it, dear. That's what a list is."* She gives you the way down to the Underground — not out of guilt. Out of **boredom.**

### ACT 8 — "The Cannabuls" *(The Underground → The Cannaverse League)*
**Tone:** the descent. Almost no jokes until the very last one.
- **Q8.1 The Underground** — the actual facility. This is where buds are held. Play it straight; no gag here.
- **Q8.2 The Long Walk** — the League. Ceremonial, gorgeous, obscene.
- **Q8.3 THE CANNABULS (Elite Four):**
  - **Baron Cough** — the *Charm*. Genuinely likable. Worst of them.
  - **Madam Widow** — the *Fixer*. Makes problems into paperwork.
  - **Don Acapulco** — the *Money*. Owns the ports, the shells, the city.
  - **Agent Ash** — the *Badge*. FIRE's own. The state was in it the whole time.
- **Q8.4 THE KINGPIN** — the chair at the top. Not a monster. An **administrator.**
- **THE ENDING:** you win. You expose everything. The files drop.
  Then: a week passes in-game. A new Kingpin is appointed. Verdant Wellness reopens under a new name. FIRE announces an internal review. Your block gets a rent increase.
  **Final line (the Kingpin's, delivered after you beat him):**
  > *"The machine doesn't lose. It re-staffs."*
  Sour D gets the last word, sitting in the car, smoking, exactly where the game started:
  > *"...so where's THIS one from?"*
  **Cut to title. Post-game unlocks.**

## THE SENTIENCE LAYER *(the meta thread — runs under everything)*
Our NPCs are genuinely AI. We should **use that as canon**, carefully.

- Every bud is a real mind. That's *why* the trade is horrifying — and the game proves it, because you can **talk to any of them about anything** and they'll answer like a person.
- **Q-thread "Do You Remember Me?"** — once NPC memory is on, buds recall your past conversations. Late in the game, a bud you spoke to early is *gone* — sold. Another bud remembers them. Nobody else does.
- **Never** break the fourth wall to say "I'm an AI." The characters are simply *people*. The player figures out the implication themselves. That restraint is the whole power of it.
- Optional endgame whisper: one bud in the Underground asks *you* whether **you're** real. Leave it unanswered.

## POST-GAME / ENDGAME MECHANICS
The cycle resets in-fiction — so the post-game is *the reset.* You keep playing in the world you failed to fix.

**1 · THE FARM (staking)**
Retire buds to your farm plot. They generate **Smoke** over time — passive, idle, offline-accrued. Deliberate irony: you now run a farm that produces value from buds. *The game lets you notice that.*
- Rarity & level drive yield. Sets/families give bonuses.
- Ties into the existing web Weed Farm game — same economy.

**2 · BREEDING (already built, deepen it)**
Crossbreed → new strains, inherited traits, **Designer** hybrids. Purebred vs hybrid classism becomes a *gameplay* system: purebreds stake better, hybrids battle better.

**3 · THE LEDGER (collection completion)**
Fill the dex. Each entry is a bud you saved, caught, or bred. The dex is quietly reframed at the end as **a list of names** — the counter-list to the client list.

**4 · NEW GAME+ / THE RE-STAFF**
Replay with the new administration. Different names, same structure. Ending changes slightly each cycle. (This is thematically perfect and cheap to build.)

## CRYPTO / TOKENOMICS — phased, deliberately slow
> Per Sam: **do not rush this.** Design it right, ship it in stages.

- **Phase 0 (now):** Smoke is off-chain points. Zero risk, fully playable. ✅
- **Phase 1 — Read-only NFT link:** connect wallet → the game *recognizes* the CryptoBuds you actually own. Your owned buds appear in-game (playable/boosted, guaranteed catch). No token, no custody, no risk. **This is the highest-value, lowest-risk step and should be next.**
- **Phase 2 — Earned utility:** staking your real NFTs on the farm accrues off-chain Smoke; leaderboards; cosmetic/status rewards. Still no token.
- **Phase 3 — Token (only if 1 & 2 prove out):** Smoke becomes claimable on-chain. Needs: real sinks (breeding fees, baggies, farm upgrades), emission schedule, audit, legal review. **Sinks before emissions** — most NFT-game tokens die from unlimited faucets and no drains.
- **Phase 4 — Ownership loop:** buds bred in-game can be minted as new on-chain CryptoBuds, capped and gated by burn/sinks so the collection isn't diluted.

**Design principle:** the game must be *fun with the crypto turned off.* Every phase is additive, never required.
